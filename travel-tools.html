BUYER'S GUIDE

Choose software by the job to be done.

Independent travel advisor

Prioritise itinerary building, client CRM, supplier access, quotes, invoicing and clear customer communication.

Tour operator

Evaluate product inventory, departures, capacity, supplier contracts, waivers, payments, booking changes and channel distribution.

Accommodation business

Assess property management, channel connectivity, housekeeping workflows, direct booking, guest messaging and revenue reporting.

TECHNICAL FIT

Check integrations before purchase.

  • Confirm supported integrations and which plan includes them.
  • Ask whether data sync is real-time, scheduled or manual.
  • Review API limits, webhooks, export options and implementation effort.
  • Check role permissions, audit logs, backups and data retention.
  • Test a realistic workflow using a trial or vendor demonstration.

Integration is not automatic

Two products may both advertise an API but still need custom development, a third-party connector or additional fees. Validate the exact workflow with each provider.

SECURITY & GOVERNANCE

Protect guest and business data.

Access control

Look for multi-factor authentication, least-privilege roles, staff offboarding and logs of sensitive changes.

Payments & privacy

Understand payment-provider responsibilities, privacy terms, data processing locations, retention and deletion processes.

Continuity

Ask about backups, incident communication, uptime commitments, recovery targets, support hours and data export if you leave.

PROCUREMENT

Compare the total cost.

Subscription

Check per-user, per-property, per-booking or usage-based pricing, plus annual commitments and renewal conditions.

Implementation

Include migration, training, onboarding, integration work, hardware and ongoing administrator time.

Evidence

Record official product links, plan checked, region, verification date and any open questions. Reconfirm features before purchasing.
