ACCOMMODATION TYPES

Find a stay that fits the trip.

Hotels & resorts

Compare location, room category, meal plan, service hours, facilities, taxes and resort fees—not just the headline nightly rate.

Homestays & guesthouses

Review host communication, shared spaces, house rules, meal arrangements, check-in windows and the level of privacy provided.

Holiday rentals

Confirm the exact address area, cleaning fees, deposits, occupancy rules, cancellation terms and whether the listing is professionally managed.

LOCATION MATTERS

Choose your base wisely.

Near the main sights

Useful for short trips and early starts, but check noise, traffic, parking and whether the area is walkable after dark.

Transit-connected

Check walking distance to the actual station entrance, luggage access, late-night service and transfer reliability.

Remote & scenic

Confirm road conditions, mobile coverage, meal availability, accessibility and what happens if weather disrupts access.

BOOKING DUE DILIGENCE

Read beyond the photos.

  • Check recent reviews for repeated issues, not one-off complaints.
  • Verify the room type, bed configuration, occupancy and view category.
  • Read cancellation, prepayment, deposit and refund rules.
  • Confirm check-in/check-out times and late-arrival procedures.
  • Ask about lift access, stairs, bathrooms and special requirements.

Price transparency

Compare the total payable amount including taxes, service fees, meals, parking, extra beds and deposits. Screenshots and confirmation emails can help resolve discrepancies.

STAY OPERATIONS

How accommodation works behind the scenes.

Reservations

Reservation and property-management systems maintain rates, room inventory, guest records and booking status.

Distribution

Channel managers and central reservation systems synchronise inventory and rates across direct channels and third-party platforms.

Guest experience

Housekeeping, maintenance, front desk, payments and guest messaging processes all shape the actual stay. A platform listing cannot guarantee service quality.
