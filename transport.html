ACCESSIBLE BY DESIGN

Build in a little breathing room.

Travel plans work better when they reflect real-world needs. Allow extra time for mobility assistance, children, older travellers, luggage, meal stops and uncertain connections.

  • Confirm accessibility support directly with the operator.
  • Keep tickets, IDs and essential contacts accessible offline.
  • Share pickup points and timing with your travel party.
  • Have a backup option for critical transfers.

COMPARE OPTIONS

Pick transport by journey, not just price.

Rail

Check station location, class, luggage rules, connection buffers, timetable changes and official ticket status.

Road

Compare realistic drive time, traffic, tolls, rest stops, road conditions, driver hours and parking at the destination.

Air & long distance

Include airport transfers, baggage, check-in time, delays and onward connection risk when comparing total travel time.

LAST-MILE ACCESS

Plan the final kilometres.

The last leg can be the most confusing part of a journey. Confirm the pickup point and the destination’s exact entrance rather than relying on a broad place name.

  • Save the pickup point and driver/operator contact details.
  • Check local taxi, shuttle, public transit and walking options.
  • Confirm luggage capacity, child seats and accessibility requirements.
  • Allow a buffer for traffic, weather and late arrivals.

When travelling remotely

Download offline maps, tell someone your route when appropriate and verify local advice on road access. Do not assume mobile coverage or transport availability.

COST & FLEXIBILITY

Understand the full fare.

Total cost

Add booking fees, baggage, seat selection, tolls, parking, transfers, fuel and possible overnight stops.

Change rules

Read refund, rescheduling, cancellation, no-show and missed-connection terms before paying.

Reliability

For time-critical plans, compare departure frequency, alternatives, disruption support and connection buffers.

TRAVEL READY

Your transport checklist.

Before departure

Verify tickets, departure terminal/platform, local time, identity requirements and operator updates from official channels.

During the journey

Keep essential documents, medication, water and contact information accessible, and follow carrier safety guidance.

On arrival

Know the next connection, accommodation check-in time and a backup option if the original transfer is delayed.
