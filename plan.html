STEP 01

Start with your trip brief.

Purpose

Write down the trip’s main purpose—rest, family time, culture, adventure, work or a special event—and what a successful trip looks like.

Constraints

Set dates, trip length, budget range, travel pace, mobility needs and must-have experiences before comparing options.

Priority list

Separate non-negotiables from nice-to-haves. This makes trade-offs easier when time, cost or availability changes.

STEP 02

Build a realistic route.

  • Group nearby sights and activities into the same day.
  • Use realistic door-to-door travel time, not just map distance.
  • Leave buffer time for meals, rest, queues and delays.
  • Check opening days, ticket requirements and seasonal access.
  • Avoid booking non-refundable activities too tightly together.

Keep the plan flexible

Make a primary plan and one sensible alternative for weather, transport disruption or fatigue. A good itinerary supports the trip; it should not make every minute feel rushed.

STEP 03

Budget the whole journey.

Transport

Include long-distance tickets, local transit, transfers, parking, tolls and baggage or seat fees.

Stay & activities

Compare total accommodation cost, taxes, meals, attraction tickets, guides, equipment and booking fees.

Contingency

Keep a reserve for disruption, medical needs, changes and unexpected local transport. Set spending priorities in advance.

STEP 04

Prepare for departure.

Documents

Check identity documents, visa or permit rules where relevant, ticket details, insurance and booking confirmations.

Health & safety

Review official destination guidance, weather, local emergency contacts, suitable clothing and any activity-specific precautions.

Offline backup

Save maps, addresses, tickets and essential contacts offline. Share the itinerary with a trusted travel companion when useful.
